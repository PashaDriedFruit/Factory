How this is applied
A lot carries a cost only once it has reached that cost's stage, so stock waiting at intake is not valued as though it had been sorted and exported.

Intake cost comes from the lot's own Intake Cost ZAR per kg where one is set from its contract. Only where that is blank does the default for its grade group apply.

Grades outside Wholes, Halves and Pieces — Unsorted WHP, Industrial, Animal Feed, Waste/Reject, and the non-macadamia grades — have no default intake rate. They are valued at processing cost only, and flagged in the Valuation report.
